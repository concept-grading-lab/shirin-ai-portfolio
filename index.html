About me

My story

My professional background combines hands-on technical expertise with experience leading multidisciplinary teams of data scientists, data engineers, and software developers. I have worked across industries including financial services, banking, public-sector analytics, and business intelligence, developing machine learning solutions that address complex business challenges.

Before pursuing my doctoral research, I worked with organisations including LTIMindtree, EMREC Technologies, and DXC Technology, gaining experience in developing and deploying machine learning models, collaborating with business stakeholders, and translating technical findings into practical, actionable insights.

I am currently pursuing my PhD at The Open University, where my research focuses on applying artificial intelligence to education, particularly automated assessment of students' written responses and personalised AI-generated feedback. My research explores how transformer-based language models can support assessment while providing meaningful opportunities for students to improve their understanding.
