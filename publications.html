Domain-Adaptive Pre-training for Automated Short Answer Grading in Conceptual Physics: Reliability, Question-Level Analysis, and Error Reduction ↗
Shirin Lade, Alistair Willis, Jonathan Nylk, and Oli Howson.
Proceedings of the 21st Workshop on Innovative Use of NLP for Building Educational Applications (BEA 2026), pp. 635–650. Association for Computational Linguistics.

This paper investigates whether adapting BERT to subject-specific physics textbook materials improves the reliability of automated short-answer grading. The findings demonstrate improvements in grading accuracy and error reduction, particularly when limited labelled training data is available, while identifying variation across assessment questions.

Nano Phylogenetic Concept: Nanoparticle Synthesis from Plant Extract as Tool for Deducing Phylogenetic Relationship ↗
Bipin D. Lade, Dayanand P. Gogle, Diskha B. Lade, and Shirin D. Lade.
International Journal of Researches in Biosciences, Agriculture and Technology (IJRBAT), Volume VI, Special Issue 3, pp. 145–149.

This interdisciplinary paper proposes using the physical characteristics of nanoparticles synthesised from plant extracts as an additional source of information for plant classification. It explores how nanoparticle characteristics could complement morphological, biochemical, and molecular evidence in phylogenetic analysis.
